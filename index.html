I build and study recommender systems that people can talk to: systems where readers say what they want in their own words, and the system tells them what it did about it.

I’m a PhD candidate at the University of Minnesota, advised by Joseph A. Konstan at GroupLens. I work on POPROX, an NSF-funded research platform that sends personalized news newsletters to real subscribers, which lets us run randomized field experiments on ranking, feedback and interface ideas instead of only offline benchmarks.

My work covers the whole loop: model design, constrained deployment, offline and online evaluation, and user studies. Before Minnesota I taught computer science at East West University in Dhaka for seven years and mentored undergraduate research in machine learning, vision and blockchain. I also build small products on the side.

Research & projects

2026

Teaching Recommenders to ListenOngoing

Dissertation · RecSys 2026 Doctoral Symposium

Bidirectional natural-language control for personalized news. Readers steer the balance and mix of their whole newsletter in plain language, and the system both acts on the request and reports back on what it did and what it couldn’t do.

  • Eliciting preferences. A five-dimension actionability rubric and a three-condition experiment (open prompt, examples with guidance, interactive LLM assistant), scored by independent raters.
  • Persistent control. A constrained LLM post-ranker over the deployed NRMS ranker, with candidate validation and automatic fallback. Evaluated in a five-week randomized field study.
  • Closing the loop. Explanations that say what changed and what could not be satisfied, with faithfulness checks.
2025–26

PIXU: personalized news image selectionCompleted

Hasan, Kluver, Knijnenburg, Konstan · RecSys 2026 BEYOND Workshop

A CLIP-based post-ranker that learns a reader’s visual preferences from click history and picks one image per article, without touching the ranking. On MIND-Large it reaches 68.1% accuracy (AUC 0.783) against 35.7% for popularity and 63.0% for the best classical baseline.

Deployed on POPROX for five weeks (163 users, 5,567 deliveries) with no ranking or stability problems. Clicks did not change significantly, and a small experience survey (n=11) showed positive but non-significant trends in satisfaction, trust and perceived relevance.

Entity-aware newsletter personalizationOngoing

POPROX · preparing a five-week user-level RCT

A four-signal recommender that fuses NRMS click similarity, topic ratings, article feedback and explicit entity preferences with reliability-weighted Reciprocal Rank Fusion, producing 5 sections of 15 unique articles.
