01 / About

Research at the intersection of people and algorithms.

I’m a PhD student working with Professor Joseph A. Konstan in the GroupLens research community. My work explores how recommender systems can move beyond passively predicting clicks toward supporting meaningful preference expression, transparency, and user agency.

At POPROX, I investigate personalized news experiences, including natural-language feedback and interfaces that let people communicate what they want from a recommender system.

Research interests

Recommender SystemsHuman–AI InteractionUser ModelingPersonalized NewsLLMsInteractive Machine Learning

I combine recommender-system modeling, user studies, and practical experimentation to investigate more useful and accountable personalization.
